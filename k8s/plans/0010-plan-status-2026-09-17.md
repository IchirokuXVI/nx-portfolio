(one CronJob per database, harvester included), the schedules in `values.yaml`, backups turned on
in `values.production.yaml` only, `k8s/helm/restore-database.sh`, the backup Secret prompts in
`k8s/bootstrap/provision-release.sh`, and section 6 of `k8s/README-new-cluster.md`.

**Still open:**

1. **The restore drill has never been run.** The header of `restore-database.sh` still reads
   "MEASURED RECOVERY TIME: Not yet measured", with placeholder rows for `luna_auth`, `luna_core`,
   `luna_catalog` and `luna_harvester`. Section 6 of the plan (delete a PVC in staging and
   restore) has no record either. A backup nobody restored is the thing the plan exists to avoid.
2. **The weekly restore check CronJob (section 4.1, marked optional) does not exist.** No template
   or value names it.
3. **The bucket lifecycle rule (30 days) and object versioning (section 3.3) are not in the repo.**
   Comments in the CronJob template and `values.yaml` say to set them on the bucket. Nothing sets
   them, and whether the bucket has them was not checked, because it is outside the repo.
