# LIDL, food leaflet of 26 October 2026, offer region 43: a review and two re-reads

Date: 2026-10-30. Leaflet `lidl-alimentacion-26-10-region43.pdf`, 55 pages, valid 26 October to
1 November 2026, PDF sha256
`31572bf43cb3580ec4f1356e6607e66a308c9dd49bf98902e0745e3309edee7a`. The working material is in
`.curation-runs/2026-11-lidl/` of the main checkout (not committed).

## The review

On 2026-10-27, Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 each read the leaflet through `--engine api`. The owner
reviewed every row where the two readings differ on the review page (`lidl-2026-10-26-region43`),
and commented on 79 rows. Rows where both models agreed were not checked.

|                   | Opus 5.5      | Sonnet 5.5   |
| ----------------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Rows              | 283           | 285          |
| Cost, time        | $3.84, 12 min | $1.67, 6 min |
| Rows marked wrong | 1             | 7            |

Sonnet's errors, and what the owner decided on each:

1. **Loyalty points read as euros** (p32). "92 puntos" under a struck "185 puntos" became price
   92 and was price 185. A product bought with points never gets a price. The tile is not a row.
2. **A tile joined with "o" left as one row** (p22, p53), although rule 15 asks for a split.
3. **"La ud sale a 0,50 €" taken as the unit price** of a multibuy (p8). The unit price is the per
   kg figure. The prompt's own label examples listed the "sale a" wording.
4. **A count taken as a size** (p29, "600 hojas" read as 600 ud). A count of sheets, bags or washes
   is not a size. `ud` is the size only when the tile compares by the piece ("El rollo sale a").
5. **A word replaced by a similar ingredient** (p30, "nduja" for "sobrasada").

Owner decisions on smaller points:

- A per gram label on a per kilo figure stays as printed and is flagged.
- `format` keeps the count of pieces in the pack ("16 cápsulas") and leaves out what the product
  does in use ("80 lavados").
- No descriptive word is dropped from a name ("sin piel"), and the brand is never repeated in it.
- One size string for several variants ("390 / 400 g") stays a null size. Which variant weighs
  which is a guess, and the printed unit price fits only one of them.

Opus's one error is a variety shown only in the photograph and not split (p37, p47).

## What changed

- **`leaflet.doubt`**, an optional key of a reading row. The model writes in one sentence what it
  was unsure of. The builder keeps the tile as read, copies the doubt to `extra.doubt`, and names it
  in a document warning on that product.
- **Three sanity checks:** a name that joins two products with "o", a per gram label whose figure is
  the price per kilo, and a whole number price with a whole number was price (the shape of a
  points tile). Run over the 2026-10-27 readings, they name all five Sonnet rows of those kinds
  (p15, p22, p32 twice, p53) and the Opus misprint on p10.
- **The LIDL prompt:** a points tile is not a row (rule 9b); the unit price is a per measure figure;
  a count is not a size; the format rule; names keep what the product is and leave out the size and
  the selling lines; the brand is never in the name; copy each word letter by letter and flag a word
  you cannot be sure of; the page strip is never a tile's date; "N variedades:" with named varieties
  is a split; a closing self check for " o " in names; rule 16 on the doubt.
- **The layout check** gets a `notes` list for what the model checked and found in order. Sonnet 5.5
  answered clean JSON with `matches: false` and listed its notes as differences ("which matches",
  "no contradiction"), which stopped the run three times on a leaflet that had not changed. The
  October run had passed only because its answer could not be parsed. `layout.md` also loses two
  September specific claims (seven tiles on the cover, a banner never repeated).

## The re-reads

Sonnet 5.5 read the same PDF again, with page images identical to those of 2026-10-27.

| Run                     | Rows | Cost  | Time    |
| ----------------------- | ---- | ----- | ------- |
| v5, first prompt round  | 282  | $1.82 | 6.1 min |
| v6, second prompt round | 287  | $1.89 | 6.0 min |

v5 fixed every targeted error. It also regressed: it priced the plain -23% A+C+E+Fruta tile (p29)
at the struck 1.69, merged the "3 variedades" tiles on p41 that the old reading had split, put
selling lines and a size into names, and copied the page strip into `validityText` on about 40
rows. The second round of rules addressed each of these.

New in v6:

- Found a split that both earlier readings missed: "Piruletas de chocolate, 2 variedades: blanco o
  con leche" (p47) is now two rows.
- "L-Casei sabores" (p22) is split into two flavour rows. The owner had accepted Opus's single row
  there. The model flagged it with a doubt.
- "Pasta rellena calabaza y ricotta" (p18) lost "y ricotta", with a doubt saying the caption and the
  pack disagree.
- One new typo, "Tallitas desmaquillantes" (p34).
- 21 doubts in all. Most of them point at tiles a person should look at (multi size tiles, the
  Patata comparison line, the "Desde 2.99" flowers).

No price differs between v6 and the old Sonnet reading, except the two points rows that are gone.
The sanity pass names nothing on v6.

## Open
