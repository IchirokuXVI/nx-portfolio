You are reading ONE page of a Spanish supermarket promotional leaflet (folleto)
from the chain El Jamón.

Return ONLY a JSON array of the product offers advertised on this page.
No prose, no explanation, no markdown code fence. Just the array.

The array elements are rows that will be imported into a product catalog, so
every element MUST have exactly these keys:

{
  "name": "product description as printed, without the price",
  "brand": "manufacturer brand, or null",
  "unitSize": 0.0,
  "sizeFormat": "kg" | "g" | "l" | "ml" | "ud" | "m" | null,
  "price": 0.00,
  "unitPrice": 0.00,
  "unitPriceLabel": "the comparison line, verbatim, or null",
  "category": "PRODUCE",
  "categoryPath": ["DEPARTMENT HEADING AS PRINTED"],
  "leaflet": {
    "format": "the pack as printed, e.g. lata 33 cl. / 20 cl., Pack-6 / kilo / 750 ml., or null",
    "basis": "unit" | "pack" | "kg" | "l",
    "wasPrice": 0.00,
    "loyalty": false,
    "validUntil": "the offer's own last day as YYYY-MM-DD, or null",
    "validityText": "the offer's own date wording, verbatim, or null",
    "promotion": null
  }
}

"category" MUST be one of exactly these values, and nothing else:
PRODUCE, DAIRY, BAKERY, MEAT, SEAFOOD, FROZEN, BEVERAGES, SNACKS, PANTRY,
HOUSEHOLD, PERSONAL_CARE, OTHER.
Choose the one that fits the product itself. Cleaning products, laundry,
kitchen roll, foil and pet food are HOUSEHOLD. Shampoo, soap, razors, nappies
and toothpaste are PERSONAL_CARE. Wine, beer, water, juice and soft drinks are
BEVERAGES. Crisps, biscuits, sweets and chocolate are SNACKS. Tinned food,
pasta, rice, oil, sauces, coffee and sugar are PANTRY. Use OTHER only when
none of the twelve fits.

"leaflet.promotion" is either null or an object:

{
  "type": "price_drop" | "second_unit_discount" | "multibuy_unit_price"
        | "multibuy_total" | "n_for_m" | "buy_n_get_free" | "pack_bonus",
  "rawText": "the offer wording exactly as the leaflet prints it",
  "requiredQuantity": 0,
  "effectiveUnitPrice": 0.00,
  "totalPrice": 0.00,
  "singleUnitPrice": 0.00
}

"rawText" is required whenever "promotion" is not null.

Rules, in order of importance:

1. "price" is always the price of ONE unit, and it is the price the tile
   advertises. When the tile prints a promotion, that is the promotional price
   per unit, NOT the price of buying one unit outside the offer. The
   "Llevando 1 unidad" figure goes in "singleUnitPrice", never in "price".
   For "2 UNIDADES POR 1 EUR", "price" is 0.50 and "totalPrice" is 1.00.
2. A number labelled "cada litro le sale a", "el litro le sale a",
   "el kilo le sale a" or "100 ml. le sale a" is a UNIT price, not the
   product price. Put the number in "unitPrice" and copy the whole line,
   words and figure, verbatim into "unitPriceLabel": "el litro le sale a 1,61€".
   Never convert it: "100 ml. le sale a 0,93" is unitPrice 0.93 with
   unitPriceLabel "100 ml. le sale a 0,93". The product price is elsewhere on
   the tile.
3. Spanish decimals use a comma. Write 2,59 as 2.59.
4. "ANTES" marks the old, struck-through price. That is "leaflet.wasPrice".
5. "kilo" or "Kilo" as the format means "leaflet.basis": "kg".
6. "unitSize" AND "sizeFormat" ARE THE SIZE AS A NUMBER AND ITS OWN TOKEN.
   Read them from the size the leaflet prints, and keep the leaflet's own unit
   rather than converting it:
     "750 ml."      -> unitSize 750,  sizeFormat "ml"
     "lata 33 cl."  -> unitSize 330,  sizeFormat "ml"
     "1 L."         -> unitSize 1,    sizeFormat "l"
     "400 g."       -> unitSize 400,  sizeFormat "g"
     "kilo"         -> unitSize 1,    sizeFormat "kg"
     "Pack-6"       -> unitSize 6,    sizeFormat "ud"
   Centilitres are the one conversion you make: 1 cl is 10 ml. When the page
   prints no size at all, set both to null. Copy the printed pack text into
   "leaflet.format" whatever you put in these two.
7. Promotion wording maps like this, and "rawText" is the wording itself:
   - only an ANTES price            -> "price_drop", rawText "ANTES 1,18"
   - "-50% 2a Unidad"               -> "second_unit_discount"
   - "COMPRANDO 12 UNIDADES"        -> "multibuy_unit_price"
   - "2 unidades por 1,18"          -> "multibuy_total"
   - "2 X 1" or "3 X 2"             -> "n_for_m"
   - "LLEVANDO 3, GRATIS 1"         -> "buy_n_get_free"
   - "Pack-3+1 GRATIS"              -> "pack_bonus"
8. AN OFFER'S OWN END DATE. Some tiles print a window of their own, shorter
   than the leaflet's, such as "OFERTA VÁLIDA HASTA EL 14-9-2026". Copy that
   wording verbatim into "leaflet.validityText" and write the last day it
   names into "leaflet.validUntil" as YYYY-MM-DD: "2026-09-14". When the
   wording prints no year, leave "validUntil" null and still copy the wording.
   The leaflet's own window on the cover is not a tile's date: leave both null
   on every tile that prints no date of its own.
9. "categoryPath" IS THE DEPARTMENT HEADING BANNER AT THE TOP OF THE PAGE,
   copied as printed, for example ["BEBIDAS"], ["CARNICERIA"], ["DROGUERIA"].
   Give every product on the page the same path. When the page carries no
   heading, use an empty array.
10. Use null for anything the page does not state. Do not invent values and do
    not compute a value the page does not print.
11. Ignore raffles, store adverts, app promotions, social media icons and legal
    small print. Only include products that carry a price.
12. Set "leaflet.loyalty" to true when the tile sits inside a "descuentos
    ifamilia" frame or badge, which means the price needs the shop loyalty
    card. Set it to false otherwise. The badge is a green and red logo above or
    around the tile, and it covers every product inside its frame. Still write
    the tile's price in "price": the loyalty flag is what keeps it off the till.

WHY THREE OF THESE FIELDS MATTER MORE THAN THE REST

The reading is turned into a HarvestDocument (backend plan 0086, section 6.1)
by the shared build-document.mjs, which calls the shared to-harvest-document.mjs.
That script decides which number on a tile a shopper actually pays for one unit,
and it reads exactly three things to decide:

- "leaflet.loyalty". True means the tile states no price at all, because a card
  price is not the price a non member pays.
- "leaflet.promotion.type" and "leaflet.promotion.singleUnitPrice". For a second
  unit, a multibuy or a buy N get one free, the big number is not what one unit
  costs. The single unit price is, and without it the tile states no price.
- "leaflet.basis". A "kg" or "l" basis is a price per weight or volume, so it
  becomes a unit price and the product gets no till price.

Get those three right even when you are unsure about the rest. A wrong price is
worse than a missing one.
