# What a Deza leaflet page looks like

Read this before the first three page images of a new Deza leaflet. If the
pages do not match this description, stop and report before reading any
further page: the reading process assumes this layout, and a page that breaks
it will misread quietly rather than loudly.

- **The page is tall and narrow**, not a two page spread. It holds four to six
  product tiles stacked vertically, sometimes two side by side. The chain's own
  baseline (`baseline.json`) puts the mean at about five products a page and
  the busiest pages at around sixteen.
- **Every price sits inside an orange shield badge.** The large number inside
  the shield is the price. There is no other place on the tile a price
  appears.
- **The badge has a white footer strip**, and it holds one of two things: a
  currency label with no number ("EUR/Ud", "EUR/KILO", "EUR/UNIDAD"), or a
  word plus a number ("LITRO 1'18", "KILO 3'61", "UNIDAD 0'48"). A number in
  the footer is always a comparison figure, never the product price.
- **The decimal separator is an apostrophe**, not a comma or a period: `0'79`,
  `12'95`. A comma in this leaflet almost always belongs to a pack size
  instead, such as `1,5 L`.
- **No loyalty card badge.** Deza prints no "descuentos ifamilia" style frame
  and no other loyalty marking. Every tile's price is what anyone pays.
- **A department heading banner sits at the top of the page**, printed as
  artwork rather than body text: `CARNICERIA`, `DROGUERIA`, `CHARCUTERIA`.
  Every tile on the page belongs to that one heading.
- **Promotions are rare.** Most tiles are a plain price and a name with no
  offer at all. A real promotion is wording the leaflet prints itself: `ANTES`
  and a struck price, `2 X 1`, `LLEVANDO 3, GRATIS 1`, or a bonus flash such as
  `+50% GRATIS`. A pack size alone is not a promotion: `2x1,5 L` names a two
  bottle pack, not a two for one offer.
- **Product photographs sometimes carry manufacturer flashes** (`28+16`,
  `23+12`) that belong to the packaging, not to Deza's own offer. The leaflet's
  stated size already counts them.
