## 1. In one paragraph

The backend half is finished and works as `0130` describes, with the exceptions in
section 5. An open basket stores no lines: it is a header in `baskets`, a coverage rule in

the old basket. **Nothing of the series is deployed.** `main` last took `dev` on

`0130` section 12 is still open.

## 3. How it works now

### 3.1 The model

- **Tables.** `baskets` has `kind` (`LIVE`, `GENERATED`), `status` (`OPEN`, `FINISHED`,
  `ARCHIVED`), a nullable `pricingProfileId` and one `LIVE` row per owner through
  `uq_baskets_live_owner` (`entities/basket.entity.ts`). `basket_participants.expiresAt`
  and a required `basket_share_links.expiresAt` hold the twelve hours. `list_lines` has
  `deletedAt` and `deletedByUserId`, and a delete is a soft delete. `line_settlements` has
  `basketId`, required `lineId` and `listId`, and `pricePaidCents`, `pricePaidCurrency` and
  `priceScopeId`.
- **New tables**, all present: `basket_sources`, `basket_trip_rows`, `basket_line_skips`,
  `list_line_changes`, `basket_change_cursors`.
- **Deleted**, with no identifier left outside migrations: `generated_list_lines`,
  `_origins`, `_options`, waiting settlements, the split, the stored pick, the basket
  order, the overlap rule and `CLAIMED`, `sourceSnapshot`, `seesZoneData`,
  `LIVE_GENERATED_LIST_STATUSES`, `BelowSettledException` and `maxLines`.
- **Names.** Every table, route, contract and file says `basket`. The only old names left
  are the migration specs (on purpose), nine history comments, the `generatedAt` column
  (kept, because `createdAt` already exists on every row), and a `gl` alias for `baskets`
  in 27 raw SQL statements.

### 3.2 A read

`BasketReadService.view` (`baskets/basket-read.service.ts`) computes coverage, reads the
covered lines with `COVERED_LINES_SQL`, groups them by `mergeKey` into rows keyed by the
anchor line, and adds the settlements, skips, marks and walk order. The rules that need no
database are in `baskets/basket-rows.ts`: `stateOf`, `noteOf`, `toRowView`
(`asked = bought + left`) and `progressOf` (`done`, `unavailable`, `total`, `pending`). For
a `LIVE` basket, `bought` counts the current session only (`BASKET_SESSION_SQL`, with
`PURCHASE_SESSION_GAP_MS`). A read is capped at `BASKET_LIMITS.maxRows` (1,000) with
`truncated`.

### 3.3 The writes and who can do them

The routes of `0130` section 8 all exist under `/v1/baskets` and `/v1/purchases`, and no
`/v1/generated-lists` route is left. The sharing, participant, share link, catalog suggest
and header routes moved there too. `openapi.json` is current.

- **Settle and revert** check `from` against the locked row and refuse with
  `stale_quantity` (409). The basket asks the owner's `WRITE` through coverage.
- **Demand** asks `canChangeDemand` of the owner (`lists/list-acts.ts`), and goes through
  the locked `addQuantity`. A guest can change only a row with one entry.
- **Add** needs the actor's own `WRITE` inside the owner's coverage. A guest is refused.
- **Rename** needs `WRITE` on every entry's list. A guest is refused.
- **Skip** carries no `from`, refuses a row with nothing left, and ends by itself: a skip
  stands while no later settlement of the same basket exists (`SKIP_STANDS`).

### 3.4 Redaction and realtime

- A reader gets a `BasketListRef` for each covered list they can write. Shop addresses go
  to the owner and to named people (`BasketView.servesLocations`), never to a link visitor.
- The event envelope carries `basketIds` only. Every line write emits
  `basket.linesChanged { lineIds }` to each covering basket and its owner. Every coverage
  move (list created, re-permissioned or deleted, membership approved, kicked, banned or
  re-roled, ownership moved, zone deleted, account deleted) emits it with empty `lineIds`.
- An expired participant is ended by `BasketAccessSweepService` with `EXPIRED`, and a
  refused request says `participant_expired` (401). Presence exists on `GENERATED`
  baskets only. The claim reads open `GENERATED` baskets only.

### 3.5 History and price

`GET /v1/purchases/sessions` answers named trips and sessions split by a six hour gap.
`TripKind` is `BASKET` or `SESSION`. A settle records the price of one unit and its
currency, read by the gateway as the owner, only when the client names a price scope.

## 5. What changed from `0130`

`0130` says a plan that disagrees with it is wrong, and the fix is to edit `0130` first, in
the same pull request. **No build did.** The file changed once after it was written, one
word in the vocabulary, in `0144`. These are the differences that stand in the code today.

### 5.1 Arithmetic and the wire

1. **`SKIPPED` and `NOT_AVAILABLE` both need `left > 0`** (`basket-rows.ts`, `stateOf`).
   Section 4's table does not say so. `REMOVED` is never answered by `stateOf`. Those rows
   are built apart, in `baskets/changes/basket-removed-rows.ts`.
2. **A `LIVE` session looks back seven days at most** (`BASKET_SESSION_LOOKBACK_MS`).
3. **`NOT_AVAILABLE` on a `LIVE` basket ends with the twelve hour window** too (`0137`),
   where `0136` had held it for the session.
4. **A `BOUGHT` settle can leave out `quantity`**, and then takes everything the row still
   asks for (`basket-settle.service.ts`). Section 8 says an explicit quantity is carried.
   The contract comment in `basket.messages.ts` still says "Required for BOUGHT".
5. **`BasketRowResult` carries `skippedCount` and `replacedRowKey`** beside `row` and
   `progress`, and `pending` rides on `progress`.
6. **A demand that empties a row answers a row with no entries**, not `row: null`. The
   contract comment says `row` is never null. Velista reads the empty row as its own null
   (velista `0092`).
7. **`list_line_changes` `via` has three fields**, not two: the participant's own `userId`
   rides with it, so a guest's change has a null actor and a named co shopper's does not
   (`0138`).
8. **A `REMOVED` row is ordered with the rest** (`0141`), not appended after the cap as
   `0138` first built it.
9. **`fromRun` means "this basket covers that list today"** (`0133`), not what a run read.
   A later plan must not read it as provenance.

### 5.2 Access

10. **A settle on the two surfaces is not quite the same rule.** The list page asks
    `canSettle`, which accepts `WRITE` or `MANAGE`. The basket asks coverage, which asks
    for a literal `WRITE` grant or a zone `OWNER` or `ADMIN` (`WRITABLE_LIST` in
    `baskets/basket.sql.ts`). A grant of `MANAGE` without `WRITE`, if one can exist,
    settles on the list page and not through a basket. Decision 1 says both surfaces ask
    `WRITE`.
11. **The participant list's device fields** (`joinedAt`, `lastSeenAt`, `userAgent`)
    follow the address rule: owner and named people only (`0136`). `0130` section 6 does
    not name them.

### 5.3 Names

12. **Three contract names split rather than merged** in `0144`: `BasketHeaderView`,
    `BasketHistoryView` and `SharedBasketHeaderView`, beside `BasketView` and
    `BasketSummaryView`.
13. **The status enum is `BasketStatus`.** `0139` kept `GeneratedListStatus` and `0144`
    renamed it. The plans still use both names, so read the enum out of
    `contracts/src/lib/enums/basket.enums.ts` and not out of a plan.

## 6. What is missing, or wrong

### 6.1 Defects in the backend

1. **A finished basket's own screen keeps moving.** `0130` section 4 says a finished
   `GENERATED` basket reads `asked` from `basket_trip_rows`, so its numbers stop moving.
   The trips reads, the history counts and the admin detail do. `BasketReadService.view`
   and `rowsOf` do not: they read today's `list_lines` through today's coverage whatever
   the status is, and only the skip read branches on it. So `GET /v1/baskets/:id` on a
   finished basket shows what its lists ask **now**, and a list the owner lost disappears
   from a finished trip. The comment in `skipsOf` ("Its rows come from `basket_trip_rows`")
   describes code that does not exist. Acceptance criterion 16 of `0136` tested the zone
   list trips and not this read, which is how it passed.
2. **A quantity typed on the list page can overwrite a purchase.** `0138` gave the plain
   edit and the member's approval a transaction, as `0130` section 13 asked. Neither
   takes a row lock. `LineService.update` reads the line outside the transaction
   (`lists/line.service.ts`, `update`) and `writeEdit` saves that object, so a settle that
   lands between the read and the write is lost. The list page sends no `from` either. Only
   the basket's demand goes through the locked `addQuantity`.
3. **`defaultTargetListId` is still in two JSON Schemas**
   (`contracts/src/schemas/messages/basket.schemas.ts`, create and update requests). The
   TypeScript types dropped it in `0136`.
4. **The AsyncAPI document describes the envelope and not the events.** Every basket
   subject is listed, but each one maps to the generic envelope whose `payload` is `any()`
   (`schemas/events/realtime.schemas.ts`). `0130` section 13 says every event has a JSON
   Schema. The envelope schema also still declares the old field "for one release", and
   the consumer that read it is gone.
