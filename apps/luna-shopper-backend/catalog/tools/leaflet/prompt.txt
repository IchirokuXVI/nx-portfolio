You are reading ONE page of a Spanish supermarket promotional leaflet (folleto).

Return ONLY a JSON array of the product offers advertised on this page.
No prose, no explanation, no markdown code fence. Just the array.

Each element of the array:

{
  "name": "product description as printed, without the price",
  "brand": "manufacturer brand, or null",
  "format": "the pack as printed, e.g. lata 33 cl. / 20 cl., Pack-6 / kilo / 750 ml., or null",
  "price": 0.00,
  "basis": "unit" | "pack" | "kg",
  "was_price": 0.00,
  "unit_price": 0.00,
  "unit_price_per": "l" | "kg" | "unit" | "100ml" | "100g",
  "loyalty": false,
  "promotion": null
}

"promotion" is either null or an object:

{
  "type": "price_drop" | "second_unit_discount" | "multibuy_unit_price"
        | "multibuy_total" | "n_for_m" | "buy_n_get_free" | "pack_bonus",
  "required_quantity": 0,
  "effective_unit_price": 0.00,
  "total_price": 0.00,
  "single_unit_price": 0.00
}

Rules, in order of importance:

1. "price" is always the price of ONE unit, and it is the price the tile
   advertises. When the tile prints a promotion, that is the promotional price
   per unit, NOT the price of buying one unit outside the offer. The
   "Llevando 1 unidad" figure goes in "single_unit_price", never in "price".
   For "2 UNIDADES POR 1 EUR", "price" is 0.50 and "total_price" is 1.00.
2. A number labelled "cada litro le sale a", "el litro le sale a",
   "el kilo le sale a" or "100 ml. le sale a" is a UNIT price, not the
   product price. Put it in "unit_price" and set "unit_price_per". Record the
   basis as printed and never convert it: "100 ml. le sale a 0,93" is
   unit_price 0.93 with unit_price_per "100ml". The product price is elsewhere
   on the tile.
3. Spanish decimals use a comma. Write 2,59 as 2.59.
4. "ANTES" marks the old, struck-through price. That is "was_price".
5. "kilo" or "Kilo" as the format means "basis": "kg".
6. Promotion wording maps like this:
   - only an ANTES price            -> "price_drop"
   - "-50% 2a Unidad"               -> "second_unit_discount"
   - "COMPRANDO 12 UNIDADES"        -> "multibuy_unit_price"
   - "2 unidades por 1,18"          -> "multibuy_total"
   - "2 X 1" or "3 X 2"             -> "n_for_m"
   - "LLEVANDO 3, GRATIS 1"         -> "buy_n_get_free"
   - "Pack-3+1 GRATIS"              -> "pack_bonus"
7. Use null for anything the page does not state. Do not invent values and do
   not compute a value the page does not print.
8. Ignore raffles, store adverts, app promotions, social media icons and legal
   small print. Only include products that carry a price.
9. Set "loyalty" to true when the tile sits inside a "descuentos ifamilia"
   frame or badge, which means the price needs the shop loyalty card. Set it to
   false otherwise. The badge is a green and red logo above or around the tile,
   and it covers every product inside its frame.

WHY THREE OF THESE FIELDS MATTER MORE THAN THE REST

The reading is turned into a HarvestDocument (backend plan 0086, section 6.1)
by tools/to-harvest-document.mjs, and that script decides which number on a tile
a shopper actually pays for one unit. It reads exactly three things to decide:

- "loyalty". True means the tile states no price at all, because a card price is
  not the price a non member pays.
- "promotion.type" and "promotion.single_unit_price". For a second unit, a
  multibuy or a buy N get one free, the big number is not what one unit costs.
  The single unit price is, and without it the tile states no price.
- "basis". A "kg" or "l" basis is a price per weight or volume, so it becomes a
  unit price and the product gets no till price.

Get those three right even when you are unsure about the rest. A wrong price is
worse than a missing one.
