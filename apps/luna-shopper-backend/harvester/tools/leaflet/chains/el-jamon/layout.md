# What an El Jamon leaflet page looks like

Read this before the first three page images of a new El Jamon leaflet. If the
pages do not match this description, stop and report before reading any
further page.

- **Tiles are denser than Deza's.** The chain's own baseline (`baseline.json`)
  puts the mean at about eight products a page. A busy page holds around
  twelve.
- **Prices use a comma as the decimal separator**: `2,59`, not an apostrophe.
- **`ANTES` marks the old, struck through price.** This leaflet runs a whole
  leaflet sale. Most tiles carry a struck price for that reason. The September
  2026 reading found one on every offer it read.
- **The tile states the price of one unit somewhere on it.** Read that number
  as the price, not the bulk or multibuy figure. `LLEVANDO 1 unidad` names
  what one unit costs outside the offer. The advertised price is the
  promotional one. For `2 UNIDADES POR 1 EUR`, the price is 0.50 and the total
  is 1.00.
- **A number labelled `cada litro le sale a`, `el kilo le sale a` or
  `100 ml. le sale a` is a comparison figure.** It is never the product
  price.
- **A loyalty badge, `descuentos ifamilia`, is a green and red frame or logo.**
  It sits around one or more tiles. Every product inside that frame needs the
  shop card. The badge is artwork. It never appears in any extracted text.
- **A department heading banner sits at the top of the page.** It is printed
  as artwork: `CARNICERIA`, `BEBIDAS`, `LACTEOS`, `DROGUERIA`, `PERFUMERIA`.
- **Some pages are flattened images with no text layer at all.** A page's
  layout gives no visible sign of which kind it is. Census the PDF first.
  Do this before you assume an engine can read a given page.
