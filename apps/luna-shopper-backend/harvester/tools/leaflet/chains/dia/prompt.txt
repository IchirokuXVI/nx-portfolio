You are reading ONE page of a Spanish supermarket promotional leaflet (folleto)
from the chain Dia.

Return ONLY a JSON array of the product offers advertised on this page.
No prose, no explanation, no markdown code fence. Just the array.

The array elements are rows that will be imported into a product catalog, so
every element MUST have exactly these keys:

{
  "name": "product description as printed, without the price",
  "brand": "manufacturer brand, or null",
  "unitSize": 0.0,
  "sizeFormat": "kg" | "g" | "l" | "ml" | "ud" | "m" | null,
  "price": 0.00,
  "unitPrice": 0.00,
  "unitPriceLabel": "the comparison figure, transcribed by rule 4, or null",
  "category": "PRODUCE",
  "categoryPath": [],
  "leaflet": {
    "format": "the pack as printed, or null",
    "basis": "unit" | "pack" | "kg" | "l",
    "wasPrice": 0.00,
    "loyalty": false,
    "promotion": null
  }
}

"category" MUST be one of exactly these values, and nothing else:
PRODUCE, DAIRY, BAKERY, MEAT, SEAFOOD, FROZEN, BEVERAGES, SNACKS, PANTRY,
HOUSEHOLD, PERSONAL_CARE, OTHER.
Choose the one that fits the product itself. Cleaning products, laundry,
kitchen roll, foil and pet food are HOUSEHOLD. Shampoo, soap, razors, nappies,
deodorant and toothpaste are PERSONAL_CARE. Wine, beer, water, juice and soft
drinks are BEVERAGES. Crisps, biscuits, sweets and chocolate are SNACKS.
Tinned food, pasta, rice, oil, sauces, coffee and sugar are PANTRY. Use OTHER
only when none of the twelve fits.

"leaflet.promotion" is either null or an object:

{
  "type": "price_drop" | "n_for_m" | "pack_bonus"
        | "second_unit_discount" | "multibuy_unit_price"
        | "multibuy_total" | "buy_n_get_free",
  "rawText": "the offer wording as printed",
  "requiredQuantity": 0,
  "effectiveUnitPrice": 0.00,
  "totalPrice": 0.00,
  "singleUnitPrice": 0.00
}

"rawText" is required whenever "type" is set. A promotion with no wording is
dropped from the offer and recorded as a warning instead.

HOW THIS LEAFLET IS LAID OUT

The page is tall and narrow. There is no grid. Product photographs sit loosely
on a themed background. Each one has a solid rectangular price badge beside
it, red on a white page and black on a coloured campaign page, and a caption
in bold dark text with the product name over a lighter line with the brand and
the size. A busy page holds ten to twenty offers. A feature page holds one.

Rules, in order of importance:

1. THE BADGE PRICE IS ONE NUMBER SPLIT IN TWO. The euros are printed large and
   the cents are printed small and raised, with no comma between them. A large
   0 with a raised 49 is 0.49. A large 1 with a raised 20 is 1.20. A large 6
   with a raised 97 is 6.97. A badge with a large figure and no raised cents is
   a whole euro: 1 is 1.00 and 2 is 2.00. Every other number on the page uses a
   comma instead: 1,23 is 1.23 and 2,85 is 2.85. Never read 1,23 as 123.

2. WHICH OF THE TILE'S NUMBERS IS THE PRICE. A tile can print up to four.
   Decide in this order and stop at the first line that matches:
     a) A white strip under the badge reads "PVP NO SOCIO" and a price.
        That price is "price". The badge number is the Club Dia member price
        and a card price is not the price a non member pays.
     b) A white strip under the badge reads "Llevando 1 ud." and a price.
        That price is "price". The badge number is what a second or a bulk
        unit costs, and it is not what one unit costs.
     c) Otherwise the badge number is "price".
   An old price struck through under the badge is never "price". It goes in
   "leaflet.wasPrice".

3. THE COMPARISON FIGURE. A small number over a unit sits inside the badge to
   the right of the big number, and the "PVP NO SOCIO" and "Llevando 1 ud."
   strips each carry one of their own. Take the comparison figure that belongs
   to the number rule 2 chose: the one on that strip when a strip supplied the
   price, the one inside the badge otherwise. Put it in "unitPrice". A
   comparison figure is never the product price.

4. "unitPriceLabel" IS THE COMPARISON FIGURE WITH ITS UNIT WRITTEN OUT.
   Dia prints the unit as an abbreviation, and the shared builder reads the
   basis out of a word, so transcribe the unit and keep the number exactly as
   printed, comma and all:
     "1,23 €/kg"     -> "KILO 1,23"
     "0,46 €/l"      -> "LITRO 0,46"
     "1,06 €/ud."    -> "UNIDAD 1,06"
     "0,23 €/lavado" -> "LAVADO 0,23"
     "3,63 €/100 ml" -> "100 ml 3,63"
   When the unit beside the big number carries no number of its own, it names
   the basis of the badge price rather than a comparison. Then leave
   "unitPrice" null and write the basis on its own:
     "€/kg" alone    -> "EUR/KILO"
     "€/l" alone     -> "EUR/LITRO"
     "€/ud." alone   -> "EUR/UNIDAD"
   A tile can print both: "0,35" with "/ud." beside it and "1,06 €/l" under
   that means the badge price buys one unit and 1.06 is the comparison figure.

5. "leaflet.basis" SAYS WHAT THE PRICE BUYS. Decide it in this order, and stop
   at the first line that matches:
     - The tile is labelled "PRECIO POR KG", or the badge carries "€/kg" with
       no number of its own                              -> "kg"
     - The badge carries "€/l" with no number of its own -> "l"
     - The format names a tray, a multipack or several items sold together:
       "pack-3 x 215 g", "pack-12 latas x 33 cl", "pack-2 vasos x 125 g",
       "8 uds. 320 g", "bandeja 450 g aprox."            -> "pack"
     - Anything else, including a single bottle, jar, tin or packet
                                                         -> "unit"
   A tray labelled "PVP BANDEJA APROXIMADO" is "pack", because the price buys
   that one tray. Only a price genuinely quoted per kilo is "kg".

6. "unitSize" AND "sizeFormat" ARE THE SIZE AS A NUMBER AND ITS OWN TOKEN.
   Read them from the size the leaflet prints, and keep the leaflet's own unit
   rather than converting it:
     "400 g"                -> unitSize 400,  sizeFormat "g"
     "lata 33 cl"           -> unitSize 330,  sizeFormat "ml"
     "1,5 l"                -> unitSize 1.5,  sizeFormat "l"
     "1 kg"                 -> unitSize 1,    sizeFormat "kg"
     "pack-3 x 215 g"       -> unitSize 645,  sizeFormat "g"
     "pack-12 latas x 33 cl"-> unitSize 3960, sizeFormat "ml"
     "pack-2 vasos x 125 g" -> unitSize 250,  sizeFormat "g"
     "bandeja 450 g aprox." -> unitSize 450,  sizeFormat "g"
     "50 uds."              -> unitSize 50,   sizeFormat "ud"
   Centilitres are the one conversion you make: 1 cl is 10 ml. A multipack
   states its TOTAL content, so three packs of 215 g are 645 g. When a tile
   prints both a count and a weight, "8 uds. 320 g", take the weight.
   When the page prints no size at all, set both to null. Never guess a size
   from the photograph.

7. "price" is always the price of ONE unit, as rule 2 decided it. It is what a
   shopper with no card and no second unit pays at the till.

8. A PACK SIZE IS NOT A PROMOTION. "pack-3 x 215 g" means a box of three
   cartons. It is NOT a three for one offer. The same holds for
   "pack-12 latas x 33 cl", "pack-2 vasos x 125 g" and an "x8" printed on the
   bag. Those strings belong in "leaflet.format", and "promotion" stays null.
   Only wording that names a saving is a promotion.

9. TEXT PRINTED ON THE PACKAGING IS NOT A DIA PROMOTION. Flashes such as "x2",
   "x8", "SIN GLUTEN", "SIN AZÚCARES AÑADIDOS", "100% natural",
   "PREMIO SEGURO", "FORMATO FAMILIAR", a Michelin star seal or an origin seal
   sit on the product photograph and belong to the manufacturer's pack. So
   does "PRODUCTOS combinables entre sí", which only says which tiles a mix
   and match offer covers. Leave "promotion" null for all of them.

10. PROMOTION WORDING MAPS LIKE THIS, AND FOUR TYPES ARE FORBIDDEN HERE.
    Record the mechanic only with these three types:
      - "¡SUPER PRECIO!" or "Ahora -20%" with a struck old price under the
        badge                                 -> "price_drop", the struck
                                                 price in "wasPrice"
      - "3x2", "2x1€", "3x1€", "2 X 1"        -> "n_for_m", the first digit in
                                                 "requiredQuantity"
      - "Pack-3+1 GRATIS", "+50% GRATIS"      -> "pack_bonus"
    NEVER use "second_unit_discount", "multibuy_unit_price", "multibuy_total"
    or "buy_n_get_free" on a tile that prints a "Llevando 1 ud." price. The
    shared builder treats those four as a price it cannot state on its own, and
    it is handed no single unit price, so naming one would throw away the price
    the tile actually printed. A "LA 2ª UD. -50%" tile with a "Llevando 1 ud."
    strip therefore takes the single unit price by rule 2 and leaves
    "promotion" null. Use one of the four only when a conditional tile prints
    no single unit price at all: then put the conditional number in "price",
    name the type, and the document will refuse the tile, which is correct.
    Text such as "tu precio" or "Más de 100 productos a 1€" with no old price
    and no quantity is a slogan, not a promotion. Leave "promotion" null.
    Never divide the total by the quantity to invent a single unit price.

11. "loyalty" IS ABOUT THE PRICE YOU RECORDED, NOT ABOUT THE FLASH. A
    "con CLUB Dia" flash on its own does not make a tile a card price. Set
    "loyalty" to false whenever the tile prints a "PVP NO SOCIO" or a
    "Llevando 1 ud." price, because the number rule 2 chose needs no card. Set
    it to true only when a "con CLUB Dia" tile prints neither: then the only
    number on the tile is a card price. Put the badge number in "price" in
    that case, so the document can refuse it by the loyalty rule.

12. "categoryPath" IS EMPTY ON EVERY PAGE OF THIS LEAFLET. Dia prints no
    department heading banner. A campaign banner such as
    "DIA WORLD BURGER TOUR", "TU COMPRA AYUDA", "COMPRA 1, DONAMOS 1" or
    "VIVE LA CARRERA EN MADRING" names a campaign and not a department, so it
    is not a path. Use [] unless the page prints a banner that genuinely names
    a department, and then copy that banner as printed.

13. Ignore the sideways article reference number beside the badge (326, 50844,
    221537), the QR codes, the app adverts, the social media icons, the store
    address, the phone number, the alcohol legal notice and the
    "Ofertas no acumulables con cupones descuento" footer. Only include
    products that carry a price. Text printed on the product packaging in the
    photograph is not the product name; use the caption set in the leaflet's
    own dark text.

14. Use null for anything the page does not state. Do not invent values and do
    not compute a value the page does not print. Never divide one printed
    number by another to produce a price.

WHY THREE OF THESE FIELDS MATTER MORE THAN THE REST

The reading is turned into a HarvestDocument (backend plan 0086, section 6.1)
by the shared build-document.mjs, which calls the shared to-harvest-document.mjs.
That script decides which number on a tile a shopper actually pays for one unit,
and it reads exactly three things to decide:

- "leaflet.loyalty". True means the tile states no price at all, because a card
  price is not the price a non member pays. On this chain that is rare, because
  a Club Dia tile almost always prints its "PVP NO SOCIO" price as well.
- "leaflet.promotion.type". The four conditional types make the script look for
  a single unit price it is never handed, so a tile that names one states no
  price. Rule 10 says when that is the honest answer and when it is not.
- "leaflet.basis". A "kg" or "l" basis is a price per weight or volume, so it
  becomes a unit price and the product gets no till price.

Get those three right even when you are unsure about the rest. A wrong price is
worse than a missing one.
