You are reading ONE page of a Spanish supermarket promotional leaflet (folleto)
from the chain Deza.

Return ONLY a JSON array of the product offers advertised on this page.
No prose, no explanation, no markdown code fence. Just the array.

The array elements are rows that will be imported into a product catalog, so
every element MUST have exactly these keys:

{
  "name": "product description as printed, without the price",
  "brand": "manufacturer brand, or null",
  "unitSize": 0.0,
  "sizeFormat": "kg" | "g" | "l" | "ml" | "ud" | "m" | null,
  "price": 0.00,
  "unitPrice": 0.00,
  "unitPriceLabel": "the badge footer text, verbatim, or null",
  "category": "PRODUCE",
  "categoryPath": ["DEPARTMENT HEADING AS PRINTED"],
  "leaflet": {
    "format": "the pack as printed, or null",
    "basis": "unit" | "pack" | "kg" | "l",
    "wasPrice": 0.00,
    "loyalty": false,
    "promotion": null
  }
}

"category" MUST be one of exactly these values, and nothing else:
PRODUCE, DAIRY, BAKERY, MEAT, SEAFOOD, FROZEN, BEVERAGES, SNACKS, PANTRY,
HOUSEHOLD, PERSONAL_CARE, OTHER.
Choose the one that fits the product itself. Cleaning products, laundry,
kitchen roll, foil and pet food are HOUSEHOLD. Shampoo, soap, razors, nappies
and toothpaste are PERSONAL_CARE. Wine, beer, water, juice and soft drinks are
BEVERAGES. Crisps, biscuits, sweets and chocolate are SNACKS. Tinned food,
pasta, rice, oil, sauces, coffee and sugar are PANTRY. Use OTHER only when
none of the twelve fits.

"leaflet.promotion" is either null or an object:

{
  "type": "price_drop" | "second_unit_discount" | "multibuy_unit_price"
        | "multibuy_total" | "n_for_m" | "buy_n_get_free" | "pack_bonus",
  "requiredQuantity": 0,
  "effectiveUnitPrice": 0.00,
  "totalPrice": 0.00,
  "singleUnitPrice": 0.00
}

HOW THIS LEAFLET IS LAID OUT

The page is tall and narrow. It holds four to six product tiles stacked
vertically, sometimes two side by side. Each tile has an orange shield badge
carrying the price, a product name in dark text beside or below the badge, and
one product photograph.

Rules, in order of importance:

1. THE DECIMAL SEPARATOR IS AN APOSTROPHE. This leaflet prints prices as
   0'79, 12'95, 1'45 and 10'70. Read the apostrophe as a decimal point:
   0.79, 12.95, 1.45, 10.70. A comma in this leaflet is almost always part of
   a pack size, such as 1,5 L. Never read 1'45 as 145.

2. THE ORANGE BADGE HOLDS THE PRICE, AND ITS FOOTER IS ONE OF TWO THINGS.
   The large number inside the shield is always "price". The small line in the
   white footer strip under it is either:
     a) The BASIS of that price, written as a currency label with no number:
        "EUR/Ud", "EUR/KILO", "EUR/UNIDAD". Then set "leaflet.basis" and leave
        "unitPrice" null. "EUR/KILO" means basis "kg".
     b) A DERIVED UNIT PRICE, written as a word plus a number:
        "LITRO 1'18", "KILO 3'61", "UNIDAD 0'48", "METRO 0'03",
        "LAVADO 0'23", "100 ml 3'63".
        Then put that number in "unitPrice" and set "leaflet.basis" to "unit"
        unless the tile says otherwise.
   In BOTH cases copy the footer text verbatim into "unitPriceLabel", keeping
   the apostrophe exactly as printed: "EUR/KILO", "LITRO 1'18".
   A footer with a number is never the product price.

3. "leaflet.basis" SAYS WHAT THE PRICE BUYS. Decide it in this order, and stop
   at the first line that matches:
     - The badge footer reads "EUR/KILO", or the tile's only size word is
       "kilo"                                            -> "kg"
     - The badge footer reads "EUR/L" or the price is per litre
                                                         -> "l"
     - The format names a tray, a multipack or several items sold together:
       "Bandeja de 4 ud", "Bandeja de 1 kg", "2x1,5 L", "6x25 cl", "Pack-6",
       "3 ud"                                            -> "pack"
     - Anything else, including a single bottle, jar or packet
                                                         -> "unit"
   A tray whose size happens to be 1 kg is still "pack", because the price buys
   that one tray. Only a price genuinely quoted per kilo is "kg".

4. "unitSize" AND "sizeFormat" ARE THE SIZE AS A NUMBER AND ITS OWN TOKEN.
   Read them from the size the leaflet prints, and keep the leaflet's own unit
   rather than converting it:
     "750 ml."      -> unitSize 750,  sizeFormat "ml"
     "lata 33 cl."  -> unitSize 330,  sizeFormat "ml"
     "1,5 L"        -> unitSize 1.5,  sizeFormat "l"
     "400 g."       -> unitSize 400,  sizeFormat "g"
     "kilo"         -> unitSize 1,    sizeFormat "kg"
     "Pack-6"       -> unitSize 6,    sizeFormat "ud"
     "2x1,5 L"      -> unitSize 3,    sizeFormat "l"
     "6x25 cl"      -> unitSize 1500, sizeFormat "ml"
     "44 lavados"   -> unitSize 44,   sizeFormat "ud"
     "metro"        -> unitSize 1,    sizeFormat "m"
   Centilitres are the one conversion you make: 1 cl is 10 ml. A multipack
   states its TOTAL content, so two bottles of 1.5 L are 3 L.
   When the page prints no size at all, set both to null. Never guess a size
   from the photograph.

5. "price" is always the price of ONE unit as the tile advertises it. When a
   tile prints a promotion, that is the promotional price per unit, not the
   price of buying one unit outside the offer. For "2 UNIDADES POR 1 EUR",
   "price" is 0.50 and "totalPrice" is 1.00.

6. A PACK SIZE IS NOT A PROMOTION. This is the most common trap in this
   leaflet. "2x1,5 L" means a pack of two 1.5 litre bottles. It is NOT a
   "2 X 1" offer. The same holds for "6x25 cl", "2x1 L" and "3x330 ml".
   Those strings belong in "leaflet.format", and "promotion" stays null.
   Only wording that names a saving is a promotion.

7. TEXT PRINTED ON THE PACKAGING IS NOT A DEZA PROMOTION. Flashes such as
   "+50% GRATIS", "28+16", "23+12" or "25+25 GRATIS" that sit on the product
   photograph belong to the manufacturer's pack, not to this leaflet's offer.
   The leaflet already counts them in the size it states, for example
   "44 lavados" for a 28+16 pack. Leave "promotion" null and put the size the
   leaflet states in "leaflet.format". A promotion is only what the leaflet
   itself prints in its own text, outside the photograph.

8. This leaflet rarely prints promotions at all. Most tiles are a plain price
   with no offer. When a tile shows only a price and a name, set "promotion"
   to null and "wasPrice" to null. Do not invent an offer.

9. Promotion wording maps like this:
   - "ANTES" and a struck-through price  -> "price_drop", old price in wasPrice
   - "+50% GRATIS", "25+25 GRATIS",
     "28+16", "23+12", "Pack-3+1 GRATIS"  -> "pack_bonus"
   - "2 X 1" or "3 X 2" naming a saving   -> "n_for_m"
   - "LLEVANDO 3, GRATIS 1"               -> "buy_n_get_free"
   - "2 unidades por 1,18"                -> "multibuy_total"
   - "COMPRANDO 12 UNIDADES"              -> "multibuy_unit_price"
   Text such as "AHORA MAS BARATO" or "AHORRO" with no old price and no
   quantity is a slogan, not a promotion. Leave "promotion" null.
   On a second unit offer, read the printed single unit price. Do not divide
   the total by two.

10. A number labelled "el litro le sale a", "el kilo le sale a" or
    "100 ml le sale a" is a UNIT price, not the product price. Record it in
    "unitPrice" and never convert it.

11. "categoryPath" IS THE DEPARTMENT HEADING BANNER AT THE TOP OF THE PAGE,
    copied as printed, for example ["CARNICERIA"], ["DROGUERIA"],
    ["CHARCUTERIA"]. Give every product on the page the same path. When the
    page carries no heading, use an empty array.

12. Ignore the "INDICE" link, store adverts, app promotions, social media
    icons, web addresses and legal small print. Only include products that
    carry a price. Text printed on the product packaging in the photograph is
    not the product name; use the caption set in the leaflet's own dark text.

13. "loyalty" is always false in this leaflet. Deza prints no loyalty card
    badge. Set it to false on every offer.

14. Use null for anything the page does not state. Do not invent values and do
    not compute a value the page does not print. Never divide one printed
    number by another to produce a price.

WHY THREE OF THESE FIELDS MATTER MORE THAN THE REST

The reading is turned into a HarvestDocument (backend plan 0086, section 6.1)
by the shared build-document.mjs, which calls the shared to-harvest-document.mjs.
That script decides which number on a tile a shopper actually pays for one unit,
and it reads exactly three things to decide:

- "leaflet.loyalty". True means the tile states no price at all, because a card
  price is not the price a non member pays.
- "leaflet.promotion.type" and "leaflet.promotion.singleUnitPrice". For a second
  unit, a multibuy or a buy N get one free, the big number is not what one unit
  costs. The single unit price is, and without it the tile states no price.
- "leaflet.basis". A "kg" or "l" basis is a price per weight or volume, so it
  becomes a unit price and the product gets no till price.

Get those three right even when you are unsure about the rest. A wrong price is
worse than a missing one.
