Price rules

  • One rule for each kind of source. When two sources price the same product, the higher rule wins.
  • A price older than the limit of its rule is out of date. A newer price from a lower rule then replaces it.
  • Turn a rule off to ignore every price from that kind of source.

Saving a rule works out the shown price of every product again. Expect a short wait.
