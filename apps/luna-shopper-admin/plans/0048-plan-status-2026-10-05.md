## 4. What 0047 found and left for the owner

None of these is scheduled. Each one needs a decision.

1. **The delete of a brand follows the old rule.** Backend `0178` changed the gateway: a brand
   that nothing uses can be deleted, and a brand in use answers `brand_in_use` with two counts.
   The back office still offers the delete only on a brand that is linked to another one, it
   still reads `brand_not_linked`, which the backend sends no more, and it has no words for
   `brand_in_use`. A new sentence and a new control are behavior, so `0047` did not touch them.
2. **`AdminSection.resources` and `provideResources` have no section that uses them.** Every
   section of the app holds its resources through `held`. Fifteen spec files still mount a resource
   through `resources`, so the field, the flat mounting of `resourceRoutes` and the fallback of
   `emptyPath` stay. To remove them, those specs must mount a `held` resource.
3. **The `PRICES` descriptor has a `list` that no screen draws.** A price is listed on the
   Prices tab of its product, by hand. `list` is a required field of every descriptor, so
   deleting it changes the contract.
4. **`harvest.sources.field.lastRunAt` is a key that only a spec reads.** The chain sources
   screen draws eleven of "the twelve" that `translations.spec.ts` lists. The key is named in
   the allow list of `no-unread-translation-key.spec.ts`.
5. **An e2e project for the back office.** `0047` section 1 says it is worth its own plan. The
   addresses stopped moving with the remodel, so this is the moment.
