### 2.4 The shop map series: 0121, 0122, 0123 and backlog 0001

- **Not built:** there is no `mocks/shop-map/`, no `ShopMapStore`, no `shops/:locationId/map`
  route and no viewer wrapper.
- **What exists:** the model library (`libs/luna-shopper/shop-map/model`, with `walkOrder` and
  `validateShopMap`, #523), the recorder library with fourteen positioning modes (#523), the
  Android walk app (#522), and the walk lab at `lab/walk` (`libs/velista/feature-walk-lab`,
  #523 and #524). The category picker 0122 reuses is built (0119, #528).
- **Missing below it:** backend 0168 (no `shop_maps` table or map route) and the editor library
  (`libs/luna-shopper/shop-map/editor` holds only its plan).
- **On hold:** the owner paused shop maps after the field test of 2026-09-28. Only admin 0037's
  header writes that down. 0121, 0122, 0123 and backend 0168 carry no status line, so each one
  reads as next in the build order.
- **0123 needs a rewrite before it is built.** The field test found that the best mode was
  absolute heading with no snapping, at a mean error of 3.4 m. Every snapping mode was 20 m to
  45 m off, and the heading settled only about 8% of the time. 0123 assumes that grid steps and
  turns (`onStep`, `onTurn`) are good enough to label aisles. It also predates the Android app
  and the walk lab, which already own sensor capture.
- **Update, later on 2026-09-29:** the owner resumed shop maps. The mock exists
  (`mocks/shop-map/`), and 0121 to 0123 were rewritten: a shop's page and the map shoppers see,
  the walks of a shop and their history, and editing by hand. Recording with the camera is the
  new 0126, 0127 fixes the walk lab, and 0128 (drawn shelves and tills) is low priority and
  built last. What this section says about 0121 to 0123 no longer applies.
