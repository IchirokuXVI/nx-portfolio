- **The shopping list by product (`0138` to `0141`).** `BasketRow` still carries `optionIds`
  and no `productId` (`libs/velista/models`). `ShopSectionsSource` is still

  (`feature-shell/src/lib/routes.ts` line 259).

  `0142` removes is all live: `basketElsewhere`, the `basket.elsewhere.caption` label, and the
  sum `bought + boughtElsewhere` in the total. `0142` reverses targets 3, 4 and 6 of `0131`,
  so the app shows the behaviour of `0131` until `0142` is built.
- **A faster basket sync (`0143`).** `REFRESH_DEBOUNCE_MS` is still 1500 in `basket-store.ts`,
  and each event still starts a read. `0143` reverses one rule of `0090`.
- **A list line with a name and a product (`0144` to `0146`).** `Line` has no `named`, in the
  models and in the contracts. The products page of a line that `0146` removes is still routed
  at `lines/:lineId/products` (`routes.ts` line 773).
- **A support session (`0133`).** Nothing outside the mock names impersonation.
- **The walk (`0136`).** No `pushGyro` and no `guard` event exist. `walk-sensors.ts` has no
  listener for the game rotation sensor.
