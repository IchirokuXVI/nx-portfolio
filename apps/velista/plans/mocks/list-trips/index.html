How to read a row

To buyThe live quantity on the reel. This is the only group where the number can change and where lines can be put in order.
Live basket and old tripsThe large number is what that trip left unbought. The small text is what it bought, out of what it asked for.
SuggestedAn existing line at zero whose rhythm says it is due. Add raises its quantity to the usual amount.

The rules this mock follows

  1. Groups are trips. A trip is a basket that drew from this list, or a run of purchases settled by hand. The first kind shows its name and date. The second shows "Loose buys" and a date.
  2. To buy comes first and never folds. It holds every wanted line that no live basket holds. Lines at zero that were never bought sit last in it.
  3. Live baskets come next. The newest starts open. Any other starts closed.
  4. Old rows are frozen and read only. The number is what that trip left. The live quantity changes in To buy alone.
  5. A suggestion is a line at zero that is due. Add puts it back at its usual amount. Nothing dismisses it in the first version.
  6. Lines are put in order inside To buy only. Every other line keeps its place.
  7. Search flattens the page. It shows every matching line once, with its reel, so a line that lives only in old trips can be found and raised again.

What loads, and when

  • The lines load in full, as today. Search, the counts and reorder all need the whole list, and a list stops growing once a household has written what it buys.
  • The trips load a page at a time. They grow every week for ever. The page reads the newest twenty labels, and "Show older trips" reads the next twenty.
  • The rows of a trip load when it opens. A closed group costs one label. Placeholder rows hold the height, so the fold opens once and does not jump.
  • Live baskets are never paged. They always arrive with the first read.

Left for later

  • A line added during a trip cannot be sent to the live basket yet.
  • A quantity raised on the list does not reach the live basket. The row says "The list now asks for 3" until the basket follows the list.
  • A suggestion cannot be dismissed.
  • A line at zero that was never bought has no better home than the end of To buy.
