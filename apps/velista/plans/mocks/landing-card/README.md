# The landing card looks like a list

The mock for `0175` and `0176`.

**This folder is not built by `build-index.mjs`.** `index.html` is written by hand, it is the
file that is published, and there is no `canvas.json` and no artboard beside it. The page
draws one landing screen in English and in Spanish at the five sizes `0176` checks, from the
same script, so each frame shows the real text lengths of its language.

The owner confirmed round 6 on 2026-10-30 (variant C, after rounds 1 to 5 in the same
session). The frames are drawn on Day with the literal colours of `0002`.

- `photos/` holds four Open Food Facts photos copied from `typeahead/photos`. They show real
  packaging. They are for the mock only, and `0175` does not ship them.
- `today/landing-prod-844.png` is production at 390 by 844 on 2026-10-30, the page this
  replaces.

The plans are the authority. Where the mock and `0175` disagree, `0175` wins.
