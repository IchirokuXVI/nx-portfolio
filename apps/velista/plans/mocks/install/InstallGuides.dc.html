android-menu · Firefox, Samsung, and the fallback

Keep Velista on your phone

It sits on your home screen like any other app, opens on its own, and your lists are there when the signal is not.

Add it from your browser menu

  1. 1 Open the browser menu, usually three dots
  2. 2 Choose Install app, or Add to Home screen
  3. 3 Confirm, and it lands on your home screen

Below this point the page is identical in all five guides: the three lines about what changes once it is installed.

desktop-chromium · only when no prompt arrived

Keep Velista on this computer

It opens in its own window, with no tabs and no address bar.

Add it from the address bar

  1. 1 Look at the right hand end of the address bar for
  2. 2 Click it, then Install
  3. 3 If it is not there, open the browser menu and look for Install Velista

This frame is the rare one. On Chromium the prompt normally arrives and the state is ready; these steps are what is behind Prefer to do it by hand?, and what shows when the prompt never comes.

desktop-safari · macOS

Keep Velista in your Dock

It opens in its own window, with no tabs and no address bar.

Add it from Safari

  1. 1 Open the File menu
  2. 2 Choose Add to Dock
  3. 3 Name it, then Add
On an older Safari this menu item is not there. The page still works in a tab, and nothing is lost by leaving it that way.

An iPad that reports itself as a Mac lands here, and these steps are wrong for it. Section 8 says to decide that case rather than let it fall out.

desktop-firefox · the only no in the design

Firefox cannot keep this as an app

Not on the desktop. Velista works perfectly in this tab, and there is nothing you are missing except the window.

If you want the window

Open velista.app in Chrome, Edge or Safari on this machine, and install it from there.

Or press Ctrl D to bookmark it, and pin the tab. Velista remembers where you were either way.

One sentence of no, then something to do. That is the whole rule, and this is the only screen in the design that has to use it.
