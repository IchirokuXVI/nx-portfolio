How the amounts work

The number beside each line here is not the number on the list. It is how many you need for 1 Bathroom things.

When you add 1 Bathroom things, the list asks for that many more of each line. When you take 1 away, it asks for that many fewer, and it stops at 0.

You can still change one line on the list by itself. If a line no longer has enough, Bathroom things goes down. When every line has enough for one more, it goes up.

Buying Bathroom things buys all of its lines at once.
