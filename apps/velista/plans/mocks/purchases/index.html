The design for plan 0095, section 3: the Bought tab of the shopping lists page. Server half: backend 0142 and 0143. Phone only, 390 by 844, Night only. Generated from the committed *.dc.html artboard and the notes in canvas.json.

Scale

AN ENTRY. A finished basket with a name reads name and date. Every session reads its date alone and is never called anything. The trailing edge is how many were bought, then the amount. An entry opens its rows in place, like a trip on the zone list, and never navigates: My lists does that.

NEVER A TOTAL THAT PRETENDS TO BE COMPLETE. All priced: the amount. Some priced: the amount and, muted, how many had no price. None priced: no amount at all, and No prices recorded. A purchase ticked by hand on a zone list names no shop, so it has no price. No grand total for the week or month: that is a different read and is not in this plan.

A ROW. The text, the list it was on when you can still read that list, the quantity, and the line amount: unit price times quantity. No price is a muted dash, read aloud as no price. A row from a list you have left keeps its amount and loses its name and list.
