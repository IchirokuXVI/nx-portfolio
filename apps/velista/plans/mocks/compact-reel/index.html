Plans 0202, 0203 and 0204. The row of 0191 with the button stepper becomes the default view; the compact view is the two line row with the reel of 0043 restored, stepping by today's rule. A picker in the header of the zone list and of the basket chooses between them. THE PICKER.

The first header action on the zone list, before the filter, as plan 0202 asks (beside the filter). Its name is "View" / "Vista". The popover is the choice frame of AnchoredPopover: 276 px, rows to its edges, the raised surface, the hairline and the second elevation. The current row carries the tick in the quiet amber and the heavier weight; that is the only accent.

THE TAPE RUNS SIDEWAYS.

As the reel of 0043 did: previous, current and next in a row, 56 px tall, growing left from the reel. It covers its own row's name and price; on a 68 px row it stays inside the row and only its shadow reaches the rows above and below. Plan 0203 frame 2 says "over the next row"; this is that overlay as it was built. A tape that runs down over the next row would be a new gesture, not a restore.

A weight writes its words on the tape: 975 g, 1 kg, 1,05 kg. The spec's 925, 950, 975, 1000, 1050 are the same steps in grams.

TWO CHANGES TO THE OLD PILL.

The restored pill had a minimum width of 52 and grew with its words, so "1,5 kg" made a wider reel than "6" and the column broke. Round 4 fixes the box at 52 by 44 with the unit under the number, so every value fits and the column holds. Everything else is the old stylesheet: the subtle border, 12 px radius, the zero style with no fill.

The tape's neighbours now print their unit at 11 px, the app's floor (it was 10 px on the round 3 board).

THE FUNNEL IS A NEW ICON.

No funnel exists in libs/velista/ui icons or in libs/shared/ui. This is a new glyph for libs/velista/ui/src/lib/icons/icons.ts, drawn at the 1.8 stroke and round joins of the header's other icons. It replaces the sliders of FilterIcon on every zone list header and on the basket's tools row.

PLEASE CONFIRM: the same change applies to the real basket filter button, which draws FilterIcon today.

Note: the comment on FilterIcon in icons.ts says the sliders were chosen over a funnel on purpose, because the filter sheet also orders and groups, and a funnel says only that rows are removed. The build would rewrite that comment.

An active filter keeps today's mark: the glyph in the quiet amber and the count over its corner.

THE REEL, FINAL (B).

52 by 44, the number over its unit: number 15 px bold, unit 11 px, the size of the "/ kg" beside the price. The build needs two tokens: --app-quantity-width 52 px and --app-quantity-height 44 px.

The pill fills the 44 px tap box the reel already had, so the compact row stays 68 px.

The widest figure, "99,95", is 38.6 px at 15 px bold; the pill's inside is 46 px, so every value fits. A count has no unit and its number sits centred in the same box; its ceiling, 99 999, prints "100" over "K".

With the reel at 52 px the name and its price share 238 px in the compact row.

THE ROW SHEET (0204).

The colours of 0201 move onto the reel: the resting number and every number on the tape are green when equal to the list's ask and amber above it. Both pass the contrast floor on the raised surface and on the tape: green 5.5 to 1 and amber 4.9 to 1 on Day, both above 8 to 1 on Night.

The label is "<list> · <ask>", the list name cut first and the ask never. No menu trigger on a row: plan 0200 dropped it. The lists' heading reads "What you bought" / "Lo que has comprado" (0201), with the text button "Lists and amounts" / "Listas y cantidades" on its right, which opens the detailed view (0200).
