TWO CHANGES TO THE OLD PILL.

The restored pill had a minimum width of 52 and grew with its words, so "1,5 kg" made a wider reel than "6" and the column broke. The box is fixed at 52 by 44 with the unit under the number, so every value fits and the column holds. Everything else is the old stylesheet: the subtle border, 12 px radius, the zero style with no fill.

The tape's neighbours print their unit at 11 px, the app's floor.

THE FUNNEL IS A NEW ICON.

No funnel exists in libs/velista/ui icons or in libs/shared/ui. This is a new glyph for libs/velista/ui/src/lib/icons/icons.ts, drawn at the 1.8 stroke and round joins of the header's other icons. It replaces the sliders of FilterIcon on every zone list header and on the basket's tools row, the real basket filter button included.

The build removes the comment on FilterIcon in icons.ts that chose the sliders over a funnel.

An active filter keeps today's mark: the glyph in the quiet amber and the count over its corner. The count badge's distance from the funnel is judged on a device after the build.

ROUND 6, THE WORDS (A, chosen by the owner).

The unit goes on the answers only; the sentence has none. A count: "ud" / "uds" with no period, "unit" / "units" in English. A weight keeps its unit everywhere, since a weight is not readable without one.

ES: "Marta ya compró 1 de más. ¿Sigues necesitando 2 o solo 1?" Answers: "Sigo necesitando" 2 uds, "Contar lo comprado de más" 1 ud.

EN: "Marta already bought 1 extra. Do you still need 2, or just 1?" Answers: "Still need" 2 units, "Count the extra" 1 unit.

When the extra covers it: "Marta ya compró 1 de más. ¿Sigues necesitando 1 o con eso basta?" with "Sigo necesitando" 1 ud and "Con eso basta"; "Marta already bought 1 extra. Do you still need 1, or is that enough?" with "Still need" 1 unit and "That's enough". Nothing says 0.

A weight: "Marta ya compró 500 g de más. ¿Sigues necesitando 1,5 kg o solo 1 kg?" with 1,5 kg and 1 kg; in English 1.5 kg.

WHO BOUGHT IT (frames 5 and 9 to 12). Two: "Marta y Luis ya compraron 2 de más." / "Marta and Luis already bought 2 extra." Three or more: "3 personas ya compraron 2 de más." / "3 people already bought 2 extra." The person raising it: "Ya compraste 1 de más." / "You already bought 1 extra." No name: "Alguien ya compró 1 de más." / "Someone already bought 1 extra." The question after it is the same in each.

The build adds "ud" / "uds" and "unit" / "units" as keys with a count, so 1 takes the singular. "de más" and "extra" avoid a noun, so the Spanish needs no gender for "huevos" or "leche".

ROUND 6, THE FRAME.

The choice frame of AnchoredPopover (276 px, rows to the edges, the raised surface, the hairline, the second elevation) with the add choice's inside: a 15 px 600 title and 48 px rows. No icon and no tint, as the existing popovers have none.

It sits wholly above or wholly below the asked row, aligned to the row's end, its pointer's tip 3 to 4 px off the row's edge: below when the row is near the top of the screen (frames 1 and 4), above when it is near the bottom (frames 2, 3 and 5), as AnchoredPopover already flips between its two sides. Measured on this board: none of the 25 popovers, pointer included, overlaps its own row's box or its control, and every pointer's centre is on the control's centre (the stepper's or the reel's).

Focus starts on the first answer, "Still need". Escape, a press outside and the back gesture close it with no answer, so the extra counts (frame 6).

A RULE FOR EVERY POPOVER THAT REFERS TO ONE CONTROL (the owner).

It has a pointer, a small arrow on its edge aimed at that control, so it cannot be taken for another row's. And it never hides what it points at: not the control, and not the row's name, price and picture.

This reaches every such popover, the disabled stepper's popover in ../basket-row-buys included.

AnchoredPopover (libs/velista/ui/src/lib/list/anchored-popover) has no pointer today: it flips between above and below, and that is all. The build adds one: a 12 px square turned 45 degrees, in the popover's own raised surface with its hairline on the two outer sides, so the pointer and the popover read as one shape. Its centre follows the anchor's centre on the side the popover opened.

WHEN IT SHOWS (the owner, ruled).

The popover opens only after the control's quiet beat has passed AND the save request has answered. A run of presses therefore asks once, about the amount the person stopped on.

By then the person may have scrolled on. If the row is no longer in view, the page scrolls to it by itself first (frame 8), placing it where the popover fits, then opens the question.

To be noticed, the row and the popover flash (frame 7), proposed in numbers: 3 pulses, each 600 ms (240 ms in, 120 ms held, 240 ms out), 200 ms apart, 2.2 s in all. The accent is the quiet amber at quiet strength: the row takes the quiet amber wash (the fill of the bar's current tab, 10 percent) and a 1.5 px outline, and the popover's border and pointer take the quiet amber text colour. No fill on the popover. Under prefers-reduced-motion nothing pulses: the accent frame holds steady for the same 2.2 s and then goes, and the scroll jumps instead of gliding.

Contrast in the accent frame: the smallest muted text (11 px) is 4.8 to 1 on the Day wash and 6.5 to 1 on the Night wash.

ROUND 6, DECIDED BY THE OWNER (2026-11-22).

1. Alternative A: one sentence, then two answers. B and C are off the board.

2. The unit goes on the answers only: "2 uds" / "2 units", "1 ud" / "1 unit", with no period. The sentence prints a count bare. A weight keeps its unit everywhere.

3. The buyer: one name, two names, a head count from three people, "you" when the person raising it bought it (also when a guest bought it and the list's owner, whom the buy names, is that person), "Alguien" / "Someone" with no name (frames 5 and 9 to 12). He reviews these words after the build.

4. With no answer, the extra counts. Leaving the page, or closing the popover without an answer, drops the question, and the line keeps the amount still needed after the extra: the smaller amount, or 0 when the extra covers it. "Still need" is the answer that changes something (frame 6).

5. After "Still need" the line does not ask again while the person stays on the page. A later raise asks again only while extra units remain that nobody counted.

6. Only the zone list rows ask: the stepper and the reel. The line page and the line sheet do not ask and do not count the extra.

7. The stepper and the reel print a count bare, as today. The unit rule is for the popover's answers only.

ROUND 7, DECIDED BY THE OWNER (2026-11-25).

1. The board is final: the question on every control that raises a line, and the amount set stands.

2. "Lists and amounts" asks about every extra, bought through this shopping list or another one. One rule everywhere, with no exception (frame 6).

3. A line that changed before the answer: "Count the extra" is refused and the person is told (frame 10).

4. An answer that arrives while the control is out of sight but still on the current page: the page scrolls to it and opens the question (frame 11).

5. An answered question is not asked again for the same extra (frame 8).

6. The shopping list's "Add or find" (frame 7) stays marked as likely to be removed.

WHERE IT GOES.

The same popover everywhere: 276 px, end aligned, its pointer on the centre of the control.

Measured on every frame of this board: no popover overlaps its control, or the row, card or table row it keeps in sight, and every pointer sits within 0.3 px of its control's centre.

Sides: the sheet above the amount row (Saved stays in sight); the line page below (no room above); a catalog card below the card; the product table above the row (every list stays in sight); the zone list below the row (the field is above); "Lists and amounts" above the row.

LIKELY TO CHANGE AND BE REMOVED (the owner).

Frame 7, the shopping list's "Add or find": for now the app opens the row's "Lists and amounts" and asks there. The plan builds it as one removable piece (velista 0208, target state 19).

STILL ON THE PAGE (velista 0208, question 3, decided).

When the answer arrives and the control is still on the current page or its open sheet, it asks. If the control is out of sight, the page scrolls it into view first, flashes it and opens the question (frame 11). If the control is no longer on the page (the sheet was closed, the person left), nothing asks and the amount set stands (frame 9).

Alternative weighed and not chosen: show the question only while part of the control is still visible, and otherwise take the default.

THE LINE CHANGED BEFORE THE ANSWER (frame 10).

The count is refused and the person is told. Proposed words: "La línea cambió antes de tu respuesta. No se ha contado lo comprado de más." / "The line changed before your answer. The extra was not counted."

The notice uses the popover's frame and pointer, and the stepper shows the line's current amount.
