Velista: a list line shows its name and its product

Every row of a list has two lines: the name the household gave the line, with the price, and the product with its brand and its size. A line with no name shows the name of its product. A line with no product asks for one. The catalog opens to choose one product from a row, and to choose the whole set of products from the line page. Plans 0144 to 0146, with backend 0207 behind them. Drawn on Day. Every product and price is a sample value. Generated from the committed *.dc.html artboards and the notes in canvas.json.

Scale

RULE R1: TWO LINES ON EVERY ROW. Line 1 is the name of the line, with the price of one unit at its end. Line 2 is the product: its name, its brand and its size.

A line with no name shows the name of its product on line 1, in the regular weight, and line 2 then holds only the brand and the size. Nothing on the row asks for a name.

RULE R2: ONE PRODUCT FOR EACH LINE. A line with several products shows its favourite, or else its cheapest, and "+2" says two more will do. The star marks a favourite.

RULE R3: ONLY A MISSING PRODUCT ASKS. The square holds a plus and line 2 says "Choose a product". Both open the catalog to choose one.

RULE R4: A STATUS WORD ENDS LINE 2. A row never grows a third line.

RULE N1: THE NAME IS TYPED ON A SHEET. One field, Save and Cancel. It opens from the sheet of a row and from the line page. An empty field saved takes the name away, and the row shows the product name again.

RULE P1: FROM A ROW, THE CATALOG CHOOSES ONE. This is the page of plan 0140: the tools and the cards of the catalog tab, a radio on each card, and a bar at the foot that names the line. "Use this product" gives the line its first product.

RULE L1: "CHANGE" ON THE HEADING OPENS THE CATALOG. The products of a line are added and removed in one place. The cards stay as plan 0135 and plan 0137 draw them.

RULE L2: A LINE WITH NO NAME SAYS SO ONCE. The title is the name of its product, and "Add a name" under the list opens the sheet of rule N1.

RULE L3: A LINE WITH NO PRODUCT keeps its heading and one sentence. "Choose a product" opens the same catalog as Change.

RULE P2: FROM THE LINE PAGE, THE CATALOG HOLDS THE WHOLE SET. The products of the line arrive ticked. With an empty search they come first, under "On this line". A tick adds a product and a press on a ticked one removes it.

RULE P3: A SEARCH SHOWS A PRODUCT OF THE LINE ONLY WHERE IT FINDS IT, in the order of the results.

RULE P4: SAVE IS ALWAYS THE SAME BUTTON. The bar counts "2 products added" and "1 product removed". With no change the bar names the line, and Save closes the page.
