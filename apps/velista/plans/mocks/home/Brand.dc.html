The sail is the list

A velista is the person sailing the boat. The mark is a small sailboat whose one big sail is a sheet of paper with handwriting on it: the shopping list is what carries you along.

The sail is deliberately oversized against the hull, so the paper reads first and the boat second. Three scribbles, each longer than the one above it, follow the widening sail and read as written lines rather than as texture.
