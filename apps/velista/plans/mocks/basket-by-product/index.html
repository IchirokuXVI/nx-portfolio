Velista: a shopping list of products

A row of the shopping list is one product, with the lists that ask for it. The headings are parent categories, or the sections of the shop you are in. A row opens to its lists, and a line can take another product from there or from the catalog. Velista plans 0138 to 0141, backend 0200 to 0202, back office 0063. Drawn on Day. Scale

A ROW IS A PRODUCT.

Each line counts as one product: its favourite, or else its cheapest. Lines with the same product make one row, and the quantity is their sum.

The caption gives the price and the lists that ask for it. A line with no product keeps its own name (Velas).

The headings are parent categories. A product under two parents is drawn under both.

AT A SHOP.

Only the sections of that shop are headings. A chain has no sections. A row that no section covers goes under its parent category, below the band.

A shop with no sections shows parent categories only.

THE FIRST BUY.

This is the shop picker the filter already has, with another title. It looks for the shop by itself only when the device already allows location. With no answer it is the plain picker.

No shop is a valid answer: the cheapest price is recorded, with no shop and no chain.

Don't ask again is kept on the device.

THE ROW SHEET.

Each entry is a list: its name, then the zone and the name of the line. Change takes that line to another product, and the number is the quantity that list asks for.

Change for all lists is a text action on the heading row.

Add it to another list is last and closed. Open, it shows the lists that hold the product at zero and the lists that do not hold it.

CHANGE.

One list of products: the products of the line and the similar ones, with no headings between them.

Keep it for next time is on when the sheet opens. On, the product becomes the favourite of the line. Off, the change holds for this trip only.

A buy already made today for the line takes the new product and its price.

THE CATALOG, TO CHOOSE.

The catalog page with its search, its two selectors and its order. It differs in four ways: a close button, the title, no bar of tabs, and a bar at the foot that names the list and the line.
