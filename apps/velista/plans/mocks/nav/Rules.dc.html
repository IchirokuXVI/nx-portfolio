THE FOUR I WOULD LEAVE IT OFF · your call
1. The front door, before anybody signs in.
2. Sign in, register, secure your account, and the mail links they send.
3. An invitation opened from a link, before the code is accepted.
4. A guest holding a shared basket.
All four are the same reason: two of the three tabs need an account, and every catalog read the app makes is refused without one. A bar that answers with a sign in screen teaches that the bar is a trap.
