0200 TARGET STATE 4. CHANGE, FINAL.

A basket row is always one product: a list that takes another product moves to that product's row. So every list here has the row's product, and no list draws a product line (picture, name, brand and size, amber). Everything else each row shows stays.

Change for all lists sits under the product, in the block it changes, when two lists or more may change.

Change product / Cambiar producto (44 px tall) sits on its own line under Bought 2 of 3, in the list's reading column (placement A, the owner's pick). It is the only control that opens one list's change sheet, and shows only on a list that may change its product; a list that may not change keeps its row one line shorter.

Fits 844 px with the description, three lists and the home indicator; nothing scrolls.

AFTER A CHANGE OF PRODUCT, FINAL.

A change of product closes Lists and amounts as well as the change sheet: the person is back on the shopping list, never on this view. That holds for Change product on one list and for Change for all lists.

On the shopping list the old product's row and the new product's row are both visible, and the list now sits on the new row.

THE UNIT PRICE LINE, FINAL.

The product block shows the catalog card's unit price line under the price: unitPriceWords, drawn as the card's .unit line (text-xs, 12 px, text-muted, tabular figures), the amount then its basis in the card's .per (text-2xs, 11 px, text-muted, 3 px before it). Bases: / kg, / L, / unit (es: / kg, / L, / unidad).

Shown on frame 2: Puleva 1,5 L at 1,79 € shows 1,19 € / L.

Hidden when it would equal the shelf price in cents: Hacendado 1 L at 0,92 € is 0,92 € / L, so frame 3 draws no line. Hidden too for a product sold by the kilo or the litre, whose price already says / kg or / L (the tomato on frame 4). A kilo piece always shows its price per kilo.
