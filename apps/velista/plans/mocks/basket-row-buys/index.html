Velista: each list of a basket row, its menu and its buys

Velista plans 0200 (a menu per list in the row sheet, and the sheet that changes a list's ask) and 0201 (each list's stepper records what was bought for that list), with backend 0273 behind the second. They ship together, so every frame draws the end state of both. Each board shows Day in English on the left and Night in Spanish on the right; the Spanish words are the proposed es keys. Nothing is new in the sheet but the trigger, the menu, the ask after the name and two colours on a number: no container, no tint, no banner. The last row of boards holds the choices, lettered: the default of each is A. The board marked Today is drawn from the source on dev, because the staging test account no longer signs in. Generated from the committed *.dc.html artboards and the notes in canvas.json.

0200, TARGET STATE 2.

A small menu under the trigger, end aligned, in the top layer: two lines of text, each with a small icon. The swap icon moves into the menu, so the act it named is still recognised.

Words are not final. EN: Change catalog product, Change requested amount. ES: Cambiar el producto del catálogo, Cambiar la cantidad pedida.

Choose the second icon in Choice 2.

0200, SPECS 1.

Básicos allows one choice, so its menu holds one line. Another list allows neither, so it draws no trigger at all and its stepper keeps the column.

The steppers line up on the right in every case, so a row without a trigger only gives its name more room.

0200, TARGET STATE 3.

A sheet of its own at rows/:rowKey/ask/:lineId. The title is the list, under it the zone and the line, then one row: Asks for and the typed stepper at the wide sheet size.

It saves as it changes, as the stepper does today, so Done only closes it. It opens without focusing the field, so no keyboard covers it until the number is pressed.

0201, TARGET STATES 2, 5, 6.

The stepper counts what this basket bought for that list in this trip. Compra semanal asks 3 and has 1: normal colour. Básicos asks 2 and has 2: the number turns green. Cocina asks 1 and has 2: the number turns amber.

Only the number changes. Above the list, Got the other 2 still settles the rest of the row.

Choose how the colours show in Choice 3.

0201, TARGET STATE 7.

Add it to another list becomes Bought for another list. Its steppers start at 0 and a press up records a buy for that list. Despensa is stepped to 1.

When the answer comes back, Despensa becomes a list of the row, Despensa · 0, with its 1 in amber, and leaves this section. A guest never sees the section.
