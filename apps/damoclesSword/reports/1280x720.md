# damoclesSword — Visual QA @ 1280×720

Reference page: **Home**. Compared: Services, About, Contact.
Cross‑resolution issues (C1–C7) are in `00-common-issues.md`; this file covers what is specific to 1280×720.

**Horizontal overflow:** none. `scrollWidth == innerWidth == 1280` on all four pages.

---

## The defining issue at this width

### 1280‑A · Navbar collapses to a hamburger + header turns dark — see **C6** 🟠
This is the big difference from 1920. At 1280 (a very common laptop width) the header is already in its **mobile** state on every page:
- The inline nav (HOME · OUR SERVICES · ABOUT US · CONTACT) is **replaced by a hamburger** icon at top‑right, despite ~1280px of room to show it.
- The header bar switches from the 1920 **white** style to a **solid dark** bar.

The breakpoint that hides the inline nav therefore sits above 1280, which feels premature — desktop users at 1280 get the phone navigation. Consistent across Home/Services/About/Contact.

### 1280‑B · Home hero PATREON/META/STEAM buttons are gone — see **C7** 🟡
On Home at 1280 the hero quick‑link stack is `display:none` (only present at 1920). The hero looks emptier than the 1920 version; those CTAs exist only in the footer here.

## Other observations at 1280

### 1280‑C · Right‑aligned title underline offset persists 🟡
As at 1920, the double underline under "WHERE WE FIT IN" (Services) and "DAMOCLE'SWORD IN THE FUTURE" (About) is offset from the right‑aligned title text (see 1920‑E). Still visible at this width.

### 1280‑D · 4‑up card grids get tight but do not break 🟡
The About "OUR VALUES" (4 cards) and Services "WHERE WE FIT IN" (4 cards) grids stay 4‑across at 1280, so columns and their (empty) image boxes become narrow. No overflow or overlap — just cramped compared with 1920. Watch this if content/images are ever added.
