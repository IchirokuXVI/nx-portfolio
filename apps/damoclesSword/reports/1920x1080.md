# damoclesSword — Visual QA @ 1920×1080

Reference page: **Home**. Compared: Services, About, Contact.
Cross‑resolution issues (C1–C7) are described in `00-common-issues.md`; this file only covers what is specific to 1920×1080 or first seen here.

**Horizontal overflow:** none. `scrollWidth == innerWidth == 1920` on all four pages. No element bleeds past the viewport at this width.

**Header:** light/white bar with the full inline nav and the black angled logo block (this is the *desktop* header — it changes at 1280, see C6).

---

## Findings at this resolution

### 1920‑A · About body copy uses the display font — see **C1** 🔴
"WHO WE ARE" and "DAMOCLE'SWORD IN THE FUTURE" paragraphs render in `Audiowide` while the "OUR VALUES" block right below them (and all of Home/Services/Contact) uses the sans‑serif system font. Most visible defect on the page.

### 1920‑B · Empty media placeholders — see **C2 / C3** 🔴🟠
At full width the blank boxes are large and obvious:
- **Home:** "Realistic Interactor" (black) and "Starlit: Ascension" video (black) are empty; only "VR Sickness Reducer" has an image.
- **About:** WHO WE ARE image (black), 4× OUR VALUES card images (grey), IN THE FUTURE image (black) — all empty.
- **Services:** 3× OUR APPROACH (black), 4× WHERE WE FIT IN (black), 2× OUR PROJECTS card images (grey) — all empty.
- **Contact:** "Starlit: Ascension" game‑card image (black) — empty.

### 1920‑C · Title casing mismatch — see **C4** 🟠
Uppercase section titles vs Title‑Case sub‑titles ("Are You Interested In Our Work?", "Contact Us", "Starlit: Ascension").

### 1920‑D · Contact page shows two differently‑laid‑out forms — see **C5** 🟠
Top publishing form = single column; bottom "Contact Us" form = Name/Affair in two columns. Same page, inconsistent.

## Other things that look out of place at 1920 (not in the original checklist)

### 1920‑E · Right‑aligned titles have an off‑centre underline 🟡
The decorative double underline under **right‑aligned** section titles is noticeably offset from the title text:
- Services → "WHERE WE FIT IN"
- About → "DAMOCLE'SWORD IN THE FUTURE"

The underline starts well to the left of where the (right‑aligned) title text begins, leaving an asymmetric gap. Left‑aligned and centred titles do not show this. Cosmetic, but it reads as a misalignment.

### 1920‑F · Heading size hierarchy is uneven across sections 🟡
Section‑level titles are not one consistent size: e.g. Services "OUR APPROACH" and "CHARACTERISTICS OF THE PROJECTS…" compute to 24px, while the sibling sub‑titles in the same sections ("Direct Contact With The Client", "Scalable And Modular", the About value‑card titles) are 18.4px. The jump between what look like peer titles is inconsistent from section to section.

### 1920‑G · Services "OUR PROJECTS" cards vs Home project cards use different chrome 🟡
The Services project cards ("REALISTIC INTERACTOR", "VR SICKNESS REDUCER") have a **filled light‑blue title bar** and a tag row (PLATFORM / ENGINE / SECTOR / EXPERIENCE). The Home "RELEVANT PROJECTS" cards for the *same* projects use a **cyan left border + underline** and no tag row. Two visual styles for the same content. (Arguably fine as different contexts, but worth noting as an inconsistency between pages.)

### 1920‑H · Both Contact "SEND" buttons — verified consistent ✅
Checked because they looked different in the scaled capture: they are **identical** (`background rgb(26,26,26)`, white text, 5px `rgb(255,203,38)` left border). No issue.
