# damoclesSword — Visual QA @ 360×800 (mobile)

Reference page: **Home**. Compared: Services, About, Contact.
Cross‑resolution issues (C1–C7) are in `00-common-issues.md`; this file covers what is specific to mobile.

**Header:** dark bar + hamburger on all pages (same mobile header as 1280 — see C6).

**Horizontal overflow note:** `document.scrollWidth` reports 360 on every page (a recent commit, *"Fix mobile horizontal-scroll on Services/About/Contact pages"*, sets the body to hide horizontal overflow). That stops the page from side‑scrolling, **but it does not fix the oversized elements — it clips them.** So over‑wide content is now *cut off and unreachable* rather than scrollable. The two elements below are still wider than 360px and are being clipped.

---

## Mobile‑specific findings

### 360‑A · Contact "WE ARE LOOKING FOR PEOPLE" table is clipped — content lost 🔴
The vacancies table (`table.vacancies-table`) is ~**554px wide** inside a 360px viewport (measured left 32 → right 586). Because horizontal overflow is hidden, the overflow is **clipped, not scrollable**:
- The **DEPARTMENT** column header is cut off mid‑word ("DEPARTMEN…").
- The entire **SHORT DESCRIPTION** column is **off‑screen and unreadable**.

So on mobile the visitor sees role names and a truncated department label, and cannot read any job description at all. This is the most serious issue at 360 — real content is inaccessible. Needs a responsive treatment (stack the table into cards, or allow the table itself to scroll inside an `overflow-x:auto` wrapper).

### 360‑B · About "WHO WE ARE" title decoration overflows the left edge 🟠
The About title component (`lib-damocles-sword-double-bordered-title` → `.title-container.right-aligned`) measures **389px wide and starts at left: −61px**, so its bordered frame extends past both edges and is clipped on the left. The title text is centred/readable, but the decorative double border is cut. Minor visually, but it is the reason About shows overflow at 360.

### 360‑C · About body copy in the display font is worst here — see **C1** 🔴
On a 360px column, the `Audiowide` body text of "WHO WE ARE" and "IN THE FUTURE" is a tall, hard‑to‑read block of decorative type, directly above/below the sans‑serif "OUR VALUES" copy. The font mismatch is most damaging at mobile width.
